Background

The story behind CederBI

I know what runs behind the scenes.

I spent a decade in restaurant operations — managing teams, scaling multi-location businesses, and learning exactly where manual systems fall apart. At 2am on a Friday, when a manager quits and you're running both locations yourself, you learn fast what needs to be systematized.

Most consultants stop there. They deliver a deck and leave you to figure it out. I didn't want that for my clients, so I learned to code.

Now I build focused internal and customer-facing software around the systems that already make your business work. Then I coach the people who own the workflow. The goal is capability inside your team—not dependency on me.

Expertise

Where I actually help

Workflow Discovery

I find the repetitive decision, handoff, or customer moment that is valuable enough to turn into a focused tool.

Internal Software

Dashboards, operational portals, and simple tools that give the right person a usable view of the work and data.

Customer-Facing Software

Useful touchpoints for inquiries, service, ordering, and communication—built around the experience your customers actually need.

Systems Coaching

I coach leaders through adoption, ownership, and feedback loops. I do not step in as the person responsible for operating the business.

The specificity matters. I am not a generic consultant or a fractional operator. I work with restaurant and hospitality leaders who have a real workflow problem and want to make their team more capable with focused software.

That niche is deliberate. It's where I've lived. It's where I know exactly what systems break and how to fix them fast.

The code matters too. Most operators hire consultants who outsource the build. I build it myself. That means your dashboard is tailored to how you actually run things. It runs on your existing tools. You own it forever. No vendor lock-in, no renegotiation when you scale.

Clear boundaries by choice. I analyze the workflow, build the product, and coach adoption. I do not take responsibility for staffing, daily execution, or every decision in your business. That boundary keeps the work focused and the ownership where it belongs.
