Background

The story behind CederBI

I know what runs behind the scenes.

I spent a decade in restaurant operations — managing teams, scaling multi-location businesses, and learning exactly where manual systems fall apart. At 2am on a Friday, when a manager quits and you're running both locations yourself, you learn fast what needs to be systematized.

Most consultants stop there. They deliver a deck and leave you to figure it out. I didn't want that for my clients, so I learned to code.

Now I build the systems, dashboards, and automations myself. Your operations stack doesn't change. Your business logic is preserved. But suddenly — you're not the bottleneck anymore.

Expertise

Where I actually help

Multi-Location Operations

When you have 2–5+ locations, consistency becomes the problem. I build systems that ensure every location runs the same way — without you managing every detail.

POS & Square Integration

Your data lives in Square, Toast, or other platforms. I connect it to dashboards, automations, and workflows so you actually see what's happening across all locations in real time.

Operational Systems

Scheduling, labor tracking, inventory management, guest communication. I don't build from scratch — I systematize what you're already doing and remove manual work.

Leadership Infrastructure

Your team needs accountability rhythms and clear decision frameworks. I help build the systems that let managers actually manage instead of asking you for permission every time.
