Background

The story behind CederBI

I know the work behind the scenes.

I spent a decade in restaurant operations—managing teams, growing multi-location businesses, and learning where manual work starts to slow good people down. When a manager leaves mid-service and you are covering both locations, you quickly see which systems need to be stronger.

I wanted to go beyond recommendations and help solve those problems directly, so I learned to code.

Now I build focused internal tools and customer experiences around the systems that already make your business work. Then I help the people using them make the improvement last.

Built for lasting ownership. I analyze the process, build the solution, and support your team through rollout. Your leaders stay close to the decisions and leave with a stronger system they understand and can carry forward.
