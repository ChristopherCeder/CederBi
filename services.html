Common Questions

How does this actually work?

How long does a Software Sprint take?
Typically 4–8 weeks from a defined scope to handoff. The goal is one useful, adopted product—not a long open-ended transformation project.
Do you replace my existing tools?
No. I integrate with what you already have — Square, Toast, Shopify, Zapier. Your team doesn't learn new software. The dashboard just pulls from your existing systems and surfaces what matters.
What if I'm not sure where to start?
Start with the Workflow Blueprint: $350 for 2–3 hours. We identify the workflow, define the smallest useful product, and decide who on your team owns it. There is no obligation to continue.
Is this just software development?
No. I'm not a dev agency. I start with your operational reality — how you actually run things. Then I build systems that fit that reality. The code is the last 30%. The first 70% is understanding your business.
Who owns the work after launch?
Your team does. The sprint includes a handoff, documentation, and coaching so a named owner can run the workflow. I can stay available for systems coaching, but I do not become your day-to-day operator.
Can you take over this function for us?
No. CederBI is built for owners and leaders who want a better tool and stronger systems—not someone else to run the department. I will analyze, build, and coach; your team makes the operating decisions and executes them.
