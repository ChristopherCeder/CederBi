Common Questions

How does this actually work?

How long does a custom build take?
Typically 4–8 weeks from a defined scope to handoff. We focus on one useful product, get it into your team's hands, and support a smooth rollout.
Do you replace my existing tools?
No. I integrate with what you already have — Square, Toast, Shopify, Zapier. Your team doesn't learn new software. The dashboard just pulls from your existing systems and surfaces what matters.
What if I'm not sure where to start?
Start with an Operations Blueprint: $350 for 2–3 hours. We identify the problem, outline the most useful first solution, and decide who on your team will guide it. There is no obligation to continue.
Is this just software development?
Software development is part of it, but the work starts with your operation—how decisions get made, where information lives, and what your team needs. The result is a tool shaped around your business, not an off-the-shelf build.
Who owns the work after launch?
Your team does. Every build includes a clear handoff, documentation, and launch support so a named owner can confidently lead the new process. The 90-Day Rollout & Results Program is available to help the new tool become part of everyday work.
What does my team need to contribute?
The best results come when one person on your team can share how the work happens today, give timely feedback, and own the new process after launch. I bring the analysis, build, and rollout support; we shape the solution together.
