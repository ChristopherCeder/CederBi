Common Questions

How does this actually work?

How long does a custom build take?
Typically 4–8 weeks from a defined scope to handoff. We focus on one useful product, get it into your team's hands, and support a smooth rollout.
Do you replace my existing tools?
Not unless replacing something is the right answer. I connect with tools such as Square, Toast, Shopify, Zapier, spreadsheets, and inboxes whenever practical. The finished system is designed to simplify the work, and I train the people who will use and maintain it.
What if I'm not sure where to start?
Start with an Operations Blueprint: $350 for 2–3 hours. We map the bottleneck, prioritize the best software opportunity, and finalize the recommended features, visual direction, and owner with you. Your written Blueprint arrives within two business days. If you begin a Custom Operations System within 30 days, the full $350 is credited toward the build.
Is this just software development?
Software development is part of it, but the work starts with your operation—how decisions get made, where information lives, and what your team needs. The result is a tool shaped around your business, not an off-the-shelf build.
Who owns the work after launch?
You do. The finished system and its business data belong to your business, with no required CederBI subscription. I provide integration support, documentation, training, and a clear handoff so your team can use it and keep it accurate. Any third-party platform or hosting fees are separate and explained before you approve the scope.
What does my team need to contribute?
You provide one decision-maker, access to the relevant tools or information, and timely feedback at a few defined checkpoints. That person helps finalize the features and visual direction, then attends the handoff so the business can confidently maintain the finished system.
